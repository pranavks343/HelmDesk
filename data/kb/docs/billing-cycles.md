title: Billing Cycles and Invoices
source: docs
---
Nimbus bills monthly or annually in advance. Your billing cycle starts on the day you first upgrade, and invoices are issued on that day each period. Seat changes are prorated: adding a seat charges the remaining fraction of the period immediately, while removing a seat credits the next invoice. Invoices are emailed to the billing contact and can be downloaded as PDF under Settings, Billing, Invoices. Taxes such as VAT or GST are added based on the billing address. If a renewal payment is declined you receive error E120 and a seven day grace period before the workspace is downgraded to Free. Annual plans receive a discount of two months. To change from monthly to annual, switch under Billing; the unused monthly balance is applied as credit. Duplicate charges should be reported to support with the invoice number.
