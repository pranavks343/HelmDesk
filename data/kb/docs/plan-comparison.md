title: Plan Comparison: Free, Pro, Enterprise
source: docs
---
The Free plan includes up to three seats, sixty API requests per minute, seven days of backups and community support. The Pro plan costs forty nine dollars per workspace per month and adds unlimited seats at eight dollars each, six hundred API requests per minute, SAML single sign-on, thirty day backups and email support with a one business day response. The Enterprise plan is priced by contract and adds SCIM provisioning, six thousand API requests per minute, ninety day backups, audit logs, a dedicated success manager and a ninety nine point nine percent uptime commitment with service credits. All plans include unlimited projects and end to end encryption. You can upgrade at any time and changes take effect immediately with prorated billing. Downgrades apply at the end of the current billing period.
