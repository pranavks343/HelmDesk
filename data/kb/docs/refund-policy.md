title: Refund Policy
source: docs
---
Nimbus offers refunds for duplicate charges, billing errors and annual plans cancelled within fourteen days of purchase. Monthly charges are refundable within seven days when the service was unusable due to an outage on our side. Refunds are returned to the original payment method within five to ten business days. Refund requests up to twenty dollars are approved automatically. Larger refunds are reviewed by a support specialist who confirms the invoice and reason before approval. Refunds are never issued for usage that already occurred, for third party marketplace fees, or after the refund window has closed. Partial refunds are prorated by unused days. To request a refund, contact support with your invoice number and a short explanation. You will receive a confirmation email once the refund has been executed.
